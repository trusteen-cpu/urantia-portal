Understanding and Helping the Growth of The Supreme Being

31 / 45

Part III of the Urantia Book – The History of Urantia – gives us sixty-three Papers of the most diverse topic imaginable, ranging from: the earth’s origins, the first humans, Lucifier, the Garden of Eden, family life, prayer, religious experience, Thought Adjusters, personality survival, and ending with the bestowals of Christ Michael.

But just before the ending of Part III, the authors present us with four papers on the Supreme: The Supreme Being, The Almighty Supreme, God the Supreme, and The Supreme and Ultimate. These papers, while enlightening and engaging, are also deeply complex with a breathtaking look at time, space, and a multi-level array of personalities.

While giving attention to all aspects of the Supreme, our discussion will focus primarily on the Supreme Being. We will ask and seek answers to two questions:

1. Why is it important for us to understand the Supreme Being? and

2. How do we, as mortals, contribute to the growth of the Supreme Being?

To begin, the Supreme Being is, by definition, an evolutionary God of experience and is still in the process of becoming. This means the Supreme Being had a beginning, is now growing, but will have no end. This is pure revelation! For the first time, the world may have a consistent philosophy with which to view the cosmos; where the infinite and the finite are woven together into a whole picture. And, from a mind perspective, the comprehension of the Supreme is the maximum understanding of deity we can achieve as evolutionary being.

To expand our understanding of this finite God requires our consideration of its source, function, and destiny; while keeping in mind the Urantia Book quote: “The narrative of human ascent from the mortal spheres of time to the divine realms of eternity…should be the supreme study of mortal man.” 40:7.4

To look at Sources, we must use a “sequence of thought” because this was before time began. All of everything began with God as the I AM; the uncaused cause of all of everything, and the original premise from which humans can form a universe frame in which to comprehend time and space, and personalities. So the God of the I AM embarked upon the great adventure of liberation…of escaping from the limitation of being all of everything. (Sound like the beginning of a pattern, here – we all want to be more than what we are!)

First, God became a father by creating the Eternal Son. Now, simultaneously, the Isle of Paradise came into existence, the stationary center of all creation. (Another key revelation in the Urantia Book is: when a being comes into existence there is always a physical abode, place of residence, for them to live and from which to function.)

Next in our sequence of thought, the Infinite Spirit came into being, co-created by the Father and Son. Simultaneously, the 21 sacred spheres in three circuits around the Isle of Paradise came into existence, and the billion perfect worlds rotating in seven circuits, also around Paradise, also came into existence.

We now have the three persons of Deity – Father, Son, and Spirit – in a unified Existential Trinity, and a Central Universes which to operate. This original Trinity is the actual source of the Supreme Being.

This is called the Age of Havona, and commonly called the First Universe Age. But before moving on to the Age of the Supreme, and the Second Universe Age, it is necessary to be aware of two additional groups of beings: the Seven Absolutes and the Architects of the Master Universe.

The Seven Absolutes are:

#1 The Universal Father (The Original source of all love)

#2 The Eternal Son (the actual Source of all Spirit)

#3 The Infinite Spirit (the actual source of all mind)

#4 Paradise (the actual source of all matter and energy)

#5 The Deity Absolute (the potential source of all Spirit)

#6 The Universal Absolute (the potential source of all Mind

#7 The Unqualified Absolute (the potential source of all energy and matter)

The Eternal Son, Infinite Spirit, and Paradise (#2, 3, 4) represent the Triodity of Actuals, while the Absolutes of the Deity, Universal, and Unqualified (#5, 6, 7) represent the Triodity of Potentials.

(The distinction between a trinity and a triodity is in a trinity the Father is present, in a triodity the Father is absent.)

These Triodities are important to understand because the entire scheme the Supreme Being and finite growth is predicated on the transference of potentials into actuals – the potentials of spirit, mind, and matter into actuals of spirit, mind, and matter.

Lastly are the Architects of the Master Universe. The Papers tell us very little about these important personalities. What we do know is they are “eventuated” beings – not created - and came into existence as a result of “God’s thinking” about his plan for the Central, Grand, and Master Universes. The Master Architects contain the “blueprints” for all creation and are the “inceptors” of these plans and work closely with the Creator Son of the local universes.

How the Supreme Being Functions in the Second Universe Age:

The second Universe Age has had a beginning in time and will someday have an end. But the Supreme Being, as an incomplete but evolving deity, had a beginning but will have no end. In time, the Supreme Being, and all the beings in the Grand Universe, will become perfected through the growth process of experience in time and space. Hence, it is deemed experiential – The God of Experience in The Age of Experience.

For clarity and to seek consistency for attempting to understand the Supreme Whole, let us briefly define the three Supreme terms below:

God the Supreme: God the Supreme is a personality created by the Paradise Trinity, and is resident on the pilot world of the seventh, or outer, circuit of the Havona Worlds. (We are reminded in the foreword of when the word “GOD” is used in the Papers it always indicates the presence of personality.) God the Supreme was actually present in Havona before the superuniverses came into existence.

The Almighty Supreme: The Almighty Supreme is the power and nonpersonal aspect of the Supreme Whole, and is resident throughout the Seven Superuniverses. It is the energy and physical reality of the Grand Universe, and is mechanical as well as a living organism; much like a human body with all its electrical and chemical reactions pulsating at any given moment. The Almighty Supreme is growing and is the overcontroller of the physical power of the grand universe.

The Supreme Mind: The Supreme Mind is from the Infinite Spirit. It joins the spirit personality of God the Supreme with the power prerogatives of the Almighty Supreme to become the essence of growth; called power-personality synthesis. The Papers tell us very little about the Supreme mind, but it does say the Supreme Mind is controlled through the Seven Master Spirits and “factualized” in Majeston – the only person created by the Supreme Being. We do know that mind ever seeks dominance over energy, with the guidance of spirit.

So, the Almighty Supreme is the power, God the Supreme is the personality, and the Supreme Being is the synthesis – and this equals the Supreme Whole - all of which is, of course, guided by the Supreme Mind.

The Supreme Being is a being, much like a human being, except this being is a God – a finite God. The source of this Being is the Paradise Trinity. The source of the growth of the Supreme Being is dependent on the Triodities. Knowing our creators and administrators are finite and imperfect helps with our coming to terms with the inconsistencies, inequalities, and paradoxes of mortal life; and is one of the answers to “Why do bad things happen?”

And although the gender identity used by the authors for Deities is typically male, the Supreme Being is several times referred to as our mother.

As in this quote: “As God is your divine Father, so is the Supreme your divine Mother…: (117:6.2)

She is not a creator, except as the parent of Majeston, and Majeston is the center of universe reflectivity; a process by which our Mother is aware of all things at all times; and the authors hint it may be the consciousness of the Supreme Being. (Sound familiar? Mothers have a way of knowing all about their children; even if miles away.) As the cosmic oversoul of the Grand Universe, we literally live and have our being in Her. No one ascending mortal will meet the Supreme Being until all ascenders meet Her together - at the moment when the seven superuniverses are settle in light and life.

Next in our down stepping toward Urantia is God the Sevenfold. God is good, and has given the mortals of time and space a seven-step ladder to get to Him. From Paradise to Urantia the seven steps are: the Father, Son, Spirit, Supreme Being, the Seven Master Spirits, the Ancients of Days, and the Creator Son/Mother Spirits. Of importance for a greater understanding of how the Supreme functions are the last three – as a group - called the Supreme Creators. These are important, so let’s review them.

The Seven Master Spirits: These unique beings were created by the Paradise Trinity and are representatives of the Infinite Spirit; each having a home on one of the seven spheres of the Infinite Spirits circling Paradise. Each Master Spirits administers to the affairs of one of the seven superuniverses and is expressive of one of the seven possible variations of the Paradise Trinity. The superuniverse of Orvonton, our home, is administered by the Master Spirit # Seven. Our soul will forever bear the mark of the Seventh Master Spirit as part of our eternal identity.

The Ancients of Days: There are three on the headquarters of each superuniverse, and are literally the midway point between Urantia and Paradise. Without doubt, the Ancients of Days are the most powerful of all director rulers in time and space. They mete out just judgment and have the final decision on the issue of eternal life or death; rulings are always unanimous and without appeal. Part of their function is to keep personality records of all the ascenders since the beginning of time in their superuniverse – hence the name, Ancients of days. (I’ve been playing racquetball so long; some of the guys there call me an Ancient of Days.) Also of interest is that they participate in the creation of Life Carriers and somehow take part in the initial life bestowal on a new planet.

The Creator Sons and Universe Mother Spirits: The Creator Sons are personalized and created by the Universal Father and the Eternal Son, and make “real” the Father’s love and the Son’s mercy to the local universe of their creation. Havona is the training grounds for these Michaels and they strive to make their own local universe in the same pattern of administration. The Universe Mother Spirits come from the Infinite Spirit, and, what the Infinite Spirit is to total creation, the Mother Spirits are to the local universes. Both the Creator Sons and the Universe Mother Spirits go out from Paradise to complete the organization of their local universe and administer to only those beings they create.

On the seven bestowal missions, a Creator Son reveals the will of each one of the Seven Master Spirits which forever identifies them with the Supreme. On no one bestowal mission does a Creator Son reveal the Supreme, but when combined, the seven bestowals are a revelation of our Supreme Mother to the local universe beings.

These three groups of Supreme Creators work with the Triodities of Potentials and the Triodities of Actuals to create the Grand Universe; populated with places and beings in the process of mutual growth with the Supreme Being toward Paradise. While the power of the Almighty Supreme combines with the personality of God the Supreme to produce the synthesis of growth of the Supreme Being toward the Outer Space levels. The Mother Supreme does not initiate any causes, but seems to be the catalyzer of all universe growth. Hence, the Supreme Being is the sum total of all finite growth.

Destiny: At some point in the distant future, when every being of will choice has chosen to do the Father’s will and every inhabited world in the Seven Superuniverses is settled in light and life, the Supreme Being as a Deity actualizes; is born as a cosmic reality. An integration with the Trinity most likely will occur. The emergence of the Supreme Being will produce the greatest fireworks imaginable. The Supreme then unites with the Corps of the Master Architects and the Corps of the Supreme Creators to form the First Experiential Trinity. So the Paradise Trinity works with the First Experiential Trinity to foster the growth of God the Ultimate in the four outer spaces levels.

#2. What is OUR role in the growth of the Supreme Being? With God the Father it is relationship that is important, with the Supreme Being it is achievement that matters. Every moral decision we make that results in action is the “food stuff” adding to the growth of the Supreme.

As we ascend the seven psychic circles we begin the slow walk in that direction. Going from the seventh circle to the first requires the functioning of our entire personality, and mastery of the circles is related to our quantitative growth; while qualitative soul growth reflects our relationship with the Father. A seventh circler may be just as close to God as a First Circler, but a First Circler will have an increased consciousness of their relationship with Mother Supreme; and, a greater awareness of cosmic citizenship. Eventually we all achieve these seven cosmic levels; on Urantia or on the Mansion Worlds. Jesus was functioning in the first psychic circle during his thirty-first year when sojourning on Mount Herman.

Next are the fruits of the spirit. “The fruits of the spirit are the substance of the Supreme as She is realizable in human experience.” (1217:6.17) The fruits of the spirit are presented in several forms in different parts of the Urantia Book, but during his eighteenth resurrection appearance at Tyre, Jesus presented the fruits of the spirit as: loving service, unselfish devotion, courageous loyalty, sincere fairness, enlightened honesty, undying hope, confiding trust, merciful ministry, unfailing goodness, forgiving tolerance, and enduring peace. Like the gospel of the Fatherhood of God and the Brotherhood of Man, these fruits of the spirit are presented in a dual expression for a reason. Perhaps it is because in singular form any one of them could be hurtful to other persons. For example, honesty alone could be hurtful to some mortals if taken to extreme. But honesty, when combined with “enlightenment” could bear much fruit. Entrance into the Kingdom by faith is free, but the Father requires of us that we bear much fruit. The Brotherhood part of the Gospel is a reflection of the growth of the Supreme Whole.

Our duty to Mother Supreme: As cosmic citizens, our temporal relation to the Supreme is the foundation for cosmic morality and as a citizen of the cosmos. This is the self-conscious, sensitivity to, and acceptance of, duty. As we grow and the Supreme grows it becomes a progressive obligation, a joyful acceptance toward a morality that transcends temporal right and wrong. We act and the Supreme reacts. It is the manifestation of truth, beauty, and goodness; and the definition of love; and, as we know, love is the desire to do good for others.(emphasis on “to do”.) The entire circuit of love is from the Father, through His sons to brother and sisters, and to the Supreme. So, let us be of good cheer as we go about being of service to our brothers and sisters – whoever they are and wherever they may be – without the left hand knowing what the right hand is doing.

The Parallels between the Supreme and mortals: The parallels between the Supreme and mortals are many. We both have a spirit and personality, and both have power and energy, and a mind aware of cosmic values. The Supreme Beings’ first creative act was in the creation of Majeston; we created, with our Thought Adjuster, a soul of potential eternal destiny. We are dependent on our Mother Supreme for growth, and She is dependent on our choices for Her growth. As with fusion – the Supreme is actually born when the seven superuniverses are settled in light and light, while we have our cosmic birth at the time of fusion with our thought adjuster.

We then become members of the mortal corps of the finaliters, and eventually reach the seventh stage of spirit progression. And, as Jesus portrayed the Universal Father to mortals on his final bestowal on Urantia, our purpose in the Age of the Ultimate will be to portray the experiential attributes of the Supreme Being to those being in the outer space level just beginning their journey to find the Father, and who will know nothing of time/space experience.

In Conclusion: We looked at the four papers on the Supreme in Part III of the Urantia Book and answered questions: #1 – why it is important to seek an expanded understanding of the Supreme Being. It is because the ascension scheme should be the supreme study of mortal man, it forces us to use a sequence of ideas to address origins, functions, and destinies, and represents to mortals our greatest understanding of Deity.

When answering question #2 – we saw that we contribute to the growth process of the Supreme Being by our achievements in moving up the Seven Psychic Circles and manifesting the fruits of the spirits in our daily lives. And this helps to fulfill our duty to Mother Supreme as universe citizens of cosmic morality.

We will still walk the path of Mother Supreme throughout our journey to Havona, and Christ Michael will be illuminating that path with his constant and loving refrain: “This is the Way.”
